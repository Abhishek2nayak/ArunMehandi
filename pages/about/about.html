About Chintu Mehandi Artist

Chintu Mehandi Artist is the best mehndi service provider across Punjab, bringing together creativity, culture, and natural beauty. With over 12+ years of experience and a team of 15+ professional mehndi artists, we craft stunning bridal, festive, and custom henna designs using 100% organic mehndi.

About Us

Punjab’s Most Trusted Mehndi Service Team

Founded by Chintu, Chintu Mehandi Artist has become a leading name in bridal mehndi services in Punjab. From traditional Indian bridal designs to Arabic, Mandala, and Baby Shower Mehndi, every stroke reflects elegance, precision, and artistry. Our team only uses natural, chemical-free henna to ensure safe application and rich, deep color.
We offer home mehndi services across Punjab for engagements, Karwa Chauth, Eid, Diwali, and other festive occasions. Our designs are a blend of tradition and innovation — making every client’s experience comfortable, memorable, and stylish.
Whether you're looking for modern fusion mehndi or intricate bridal patterns, our experienced artists bring your imagination to life with passion and professionalism.
